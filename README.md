##Kun's Dream Journey

Starlight Loop is a 2D psychological horror, mystery, and puzzle-adventure game. The player 
controls Kunkun, a trainee who enters a cheerful place known as the “Starlight Training Camp.” 
Although the camp initially appears friendly and welcoming, strange events gradually reveal that 
something is wrong.  

The game contains four levels that combine rhythm gameplay, environmental exploration, 
stealth, puzzle-solving, evidence collection, and branching story decisions. The player must 
investigate the disappearance of Kunkun’s roommate, Ricardo Milos, discover the truth behind 
the training camp, and decide how to escape. 

Hidden evidence collected during earlier levels affects the available story route and ending. 
Depending on the player’s actions, Kunkun may forget everything and repeat the cycle, 
reincarnate with fragments of his memories, or survive and escape the training camp.
